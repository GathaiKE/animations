About Us

Driving Innovation Across the Savannah

Founded in Nairobi, Techy Savannah was born from a vision to harness technology to solve Africa's unique challenges. Our team of engineers, designers, and visionaries are committed to building solutions that work for the diverse ecosystems of Sub-Saharan Africa.

We believe in the power of technology to transform communities, bridge digital divides, and create sustainable economic opportunities across the continent.

Our Mission

To empower communities across Sub-Saharan Africa through innovative, locally-relevant technology solutions that drive sustainable development and digital inclusion.

Our Vision

To create a connected digital ecosystem across Africa where technology bridges geographical and economic divides, unlocking the continent's full potential.
